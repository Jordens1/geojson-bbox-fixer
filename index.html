THE SHORT VERSION

One focused fix.
No quiet edits.

We inspect bbox members on the root, features and geometries. A box that differs from the tight calculated extent is flagged—even when its larger bounds may still be valid GeoJSON.

What this checker does

  • Checks 2D and 3D positions in WGS84 longitude / latitude order.
  • Handles already-cut geometry on both sides of the antimeridian.
  • Changes only existing bbox values, or adds a root bbox when you ask.

What it doesn’t do

It does not fix coordinates, projection, polygon topology or invalid shapes, and it is not a full GeoJSON validator.
